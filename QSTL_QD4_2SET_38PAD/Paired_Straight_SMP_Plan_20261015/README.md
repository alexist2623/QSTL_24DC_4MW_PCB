# Straight two-SMP groups

Follow the user-marked grouping: each side has four pairs. End pairs are diagonal; the two middle pairs are vertical. The plan keeps the 32.60 x 77.50 mm outline, QD and its pads, ZIF, mounting holes, channel assignments and four trial central R/C pairs.

Nominal unrotated 5.50 mm square SMP envelopes have 0.50 mm clearance within each pair and 2.00 mm between an end pair and its adjacent middle pair. These are assistant-selected trial distances. The 6.60 mm QD-side band and 8.50 mm longitudinal SMP-free strip are retained. End-pair centre spacing is about 7.08 mm at 32.0 degrees to horizontal; vertical-pair centre spacing is 6.00 mm. The footprint orientations themselves are unchanged.
