# QSTL 24DC / 4MW PCB

Open `QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB.PrjPcb` in Altium Designer.

The original 19.5 x 67.9 mm six-layer PCB has four active RF channels and 20 DC-only QD connections. RF now passes directly through each R1-4 RF-side pad/via on its way from C1-4 to QD. The former separate resistor branches have been removed. Capacitors lie along the RF path, and resistors extend upward from it. Adjacent R/C copper-pad edges are approximately 0.214 mm apart. All passive rotations are multiples of 45 degrees. [RF-only view](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/RF_only.png) and [native geometry audit](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/RF_stubless.json) show this revision.

R is 0603/10 kohm; C is 0402/1 nF. QD and all R/C are on Bottom; ZIF and all eight SMPs are on Top. SMP positions, six mechanical holes, the board outline and the QD footprint are unchanged. QD's inner pad-end opening is 4.3 x 4.3 mm, with 1.0 x 0.5 mm pads and 0.7 mm pitch; pads 1 and 24 are on the upper edge.

The electrical mapping remains SMP1/2/3/4 through C1/2/3/4 to QD9/6/1/24, with R1/2/3/4 supplying DC bias at those same RF nets. ZIF contacts 1-12 and 15-26 are used. DC assignments now use ZIF17 to QD3, ZIF19 to QD2 and ZIF21 to R1 pin2 (QD9 bias), moving the R1 branch to the outside of its routing bundle. The original SchDoc and QD symbol pin names in SchLib have been updated to match; project membership and footprint geometry are unchanged. [Pin mapping](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/PIN_MAPPING.csv) records the connections.

The left placement drafts R5/C5 and R6/C6 follow the same resistor-above-capacitor arrangement. Their signal pads and SMP5/6 centre contacts remain unassigned, without new signal routes or vias. SMP7/8 centre contacts also remain NC; SMP shield pads are GND.

From the QD side to the opposite side the stack is **RF / GND / DC / GND / DC / GND**: L6 Bottom RF, L5 GND, L4 DC_B, L3 GND, L2 DC_A, and L1 Top GND. RF runs on the same Bottom surface as QD and R/C. DC uses overlapping L2/L4 routes through the centre of the board. Each layer has 12 parallel routes at the same XY positions over y=18-30 mm, spanning x=6.15-12.75 mm. The pitch is 0.6 mm and the copper edge gap is approximately 0.475 mm. The full design retains the 0.15 mm clearance rule; the measured DC trace-to-trace minimum on the same layer is approximately 0.175 mm. Crossings between different layers are intentional; through-hole pads and vias remain obstacles on both layers. The retained 0.5/0.25 mm through via at (13.35,16.4) changes ZIF23 from a short L4 escape to its main L2 route. No vias were added in this revision. The QD branches now stay in the central bundle until y=35.1 mm, close to QD. R1 bias branches from the outside lane, so unrelated DC traces no longer weave through the R1/C1 via field. The minimum copper gap between those vias and unrelated DC is approximately 0.588 mm. This removes 64 internal track segments (305 to 241) and about 2.51 mm of total planar DC route length. DC width is 0.125 mm and Top escapes are 0.15 mm. Top ZIF escapes, RF copper, component placement and stack dimensions remain unchanged. [Routing comparison](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/DC_direct_comparison.png), [DC audit](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/DC_direct.json), [DC-only view](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/DC_only.png) and [separated routing layers](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/Routing_layers.png) show the result. Straight tracks are horizontal, vertical or 45 degrees; DC changes direction by at most 45 degrees per corner, and RF uses tangent 0.3 mm-radius arcs. DC paths avoid the fixed passive and QD vias. Signals avoid the QD opening. RF delay matching remains deferred by the user; [native RF lengths](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/RF_delay.json) do not claim a 1 ps match.

RF is now an outer-layer transmission line. The copper width, dielectric materials and thicknesses were preserved in this layer reassignment; 50-ohm impedance has **not** been verified for the new surface routing and must be calculated against the fabrication stack and solder mask.

All 65 signal vias retain Top/Bottom tent flags and 130 explicit paste apertures. The 24 QD and 16 active R/C vias remain centred in their pads; Bottom component-pad mask openings expose those via-in-pad holes. No shield/stitching vias are present. Three GND polygons on L1/L3/L5 have been repoured; the former Bottom GND polygon was removed.

After save/reopen, Altium reports zero violations across 13 enabled DRC rules. A subsequent connection audit found three obsolete ratsnest lines retained after the ZIF17/19/21 pin reassignment. Rebuilding all 33 nets removed these without changing copper, pads, vias or net assignments. An independent layer-aware copper graph verifies all 24 DC nets, eight RF named nets and GND as continuous; native connection counts remain zero after save/reopen and DRC. [Connection audit](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/connection_validation.json) records both physical continuity and the retained-connection check. [Validation](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/validation.json) and [DRC](QSTL_24DC_4MW_PCB/docs/Connections_DRC.html) correspond to the current saved board. Earlier DRC and symmetry reports are historical. Current PCB pins agree with the updated schematic's 33 named nets and 41 intentional singleton NC nets. The native schematic compiler API still returns `False` with zero reported violations, as in the preceding revision; its complete exported connectivity agrees with the saved PCB. This Boolean is retained in the validation report rather than treated as a successful compiler result.
